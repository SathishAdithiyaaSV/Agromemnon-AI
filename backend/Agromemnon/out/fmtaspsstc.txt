Scheme: Fodder Minikits under Tribal Area Sub Plan (TASP) Scheme (ST Category)

Description:
The scheme “Fodder Minikits under Tribal Area Sub Plan (TASP) Scheme” is implemented by the Directorate of Animal Husbandry, Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, improved varieties of fodder seeds are provided to beneficiaries for widespread publicity, demonstration and adaptation by farmers.

Benefits:
Under this scheme, the fodder seed minikit of 3 Kg, 7 Kg or 10 Kg is given to the eligible beneficiary.

Eligibility Criteria:
The beneficiary should be a permanent resident of Gujarat. The beneficiary of the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category can avail the benefit under this scheme. The beneficiary should possess 10 Guntha of land to his or her name.

How to Apply:
To avail the benefits of the scheme, the beneficiary may contact a nearby Veterinary Dispensary or First Aid Veterinary Centre.

Required Documents:
1.	Identity proof 2.	Proof of Residence 3.	Proof of possessing10 Guntha of land 4.	Any other documents as required

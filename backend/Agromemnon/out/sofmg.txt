Scheme: Supply of Fodder Minikit

Description:
The "Supply of Fodder Minikit" scheme has been introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat. Under this scheme, improved variety of fodder seeds are provided to beneficiaries for wide spread publicity, demonstration and adaptation by farmers.

Benefits:
﻿ Name of Component Standard of Assistance Minimum Time Limit(In Year) for Reclaiming Benefits Supply of Fodder Minikit under General People, Schedule Caste People and Schedule Tribe People. Free 1 Year ﻿

Eligibility Criteria:
All farmers belonging to the General, Scheduled Caste (SC), and Scheduled Tribe (ST) categories are eligible. The applicant must own agricultural land.

How to Apply:
Step 1: The beneficiary must visit the nearest Veterinary Dispensary, First Aid Veterinary Centre, or Intensive Cattle Development Programme Office. Step 2: Obtain and submit the physical application form along with the required documents. Step 3: The concerned authority will verify eligibility and distribute the fodder minikits accordingly.

Required Documents:
7/12 and 8-a Certificate Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe cast Certificate.

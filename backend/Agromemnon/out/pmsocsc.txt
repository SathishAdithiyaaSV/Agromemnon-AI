Scheme: Post Matric Students of Class 11th &12th ( Scheduled Caste)

Description:
Launched in 1976-77, the "Post Matric Students of Class 11th & 12th" scheme is a welfare initiative by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides reimbursement of scholarship fees to students in Class 11th and 12th. It aims to encourage students from the Scheduled Caste category in the field of education and provide them with financial assistance.

Benefits:
The facility of reimbursement of scholarship fee is provided to the students of class 11th and 12th.

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category. The student should be enrolled in Class 11th or 12th in a recognized educational institution. The student should provide a valid income certificate of the family, confirming that the annual income is below the prescribed limit. The guardian should provide a declaration stating that there has been no increase in the family income for the last financial year.

How to Apply:
Registration Process: Step1: Go to the official State Scholarship Portal 2.0 for Madhya Pradesh. Step2: Scroll down the page and find the "Student Corner" section on the left side of the screen. Step3: Under the Student Corner, click on the “Student Registration” option. Step4: Complete the online registration form by filling in all mandatory fields and uploading the required documents in the specified format and size. Step5: Carefully review the details entered and the documents uploaded. Once you have verified everything, click on the "Submit" button. Step6: After submitting, you will receive your registration number on the registered email address or mobile number. ﻿ Application Process: Step1: Go to the State Scholarship Portal 2.0 for Madhya Pradesh. Step2: Scroll down to the "Student Corner" section on the left and click on "Student Login." Enter your User ID and Password to log in. Step3: After logging in, a dashboard will appear in front of you. From your dashboard, select the scheme you wish to apply for and click on it. Step4: Complete the application form by filling in all required fields and uploading necessary documents in the specified format and size. Step5: Double-check the information and documents provided, then click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.

Required Documents:
Aadhaar Card Caste Certificate Income Certificate Bank Account Details Passport-sized Photograph Educational Documents Self-attested Documents Proof of Admission Income Declaration Bank Details The student should submit original academic documents, enrollment verification, and a valid residence certificate issued by the tehsil or sub-tehsil authority. ﻿

Scheme: Mahtari Vandan Yojana

Description:
The scheme "Mahatari Vandan Yojana" by the Government of Chhattisgarh aims to provide financial assistance and support to eligible beneficiaries for socio-economic empowerment. The scheme offers monetary benefits and access to essential services, targeting underserved communities. Key eligibility criteria include residency in Chhattisgarh and possession of an EPIC Card. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, and applications are accepted online through the official portal.

Benefits:
Each eligible woman will receive ₹1000/- per month. Women receiving less than ₹1000/- per month under social assistance programs and various pension schemes through the Samaj Kalyan Vibhag will receive additional assistance to a total ₹1,000/- per month. The assistance will be directly deposited into the applicant's Aadhaar-linked DBT-based bank account.

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant must be a resident of Chhattisgarh. The applicant must be a married woman. The applicant must be at least 21 years old as of 1st January of the year of application. The applicant can also be a widow / divorced / abandoned woman.

How to Apply:
Step 1: Visit the "Mahtari Vandan Yojana" portal, c lick on the registration link. In the registration form, provide required details such as name, contact information, and EPIC Card number. Step 2: Use the credentials you created during registration to log in. Navigate to the online form of the scheme. In the form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size. Carefully review all the information provided and the documents uploaded. Make any necessary corrections. Step 3: Click "Submit" to submit your application. Save the application reference number or confirmation message for future tracking. Post-Application Processes Step 1: After the last date of application, a provisional list of applicants will be displayed on the portal/app. A printout of the list will also be displayed on the notice board at the Gram Panchayat/ward level. Step 2: The Gram Sachiv/ward in-charge will issue an approval letter to the eligible beneficiaries in the final list. This list will also be displayed on the portal.

Required Documents:
Self-attested passport-size photo Local residence document (residence certificate/ration card/voter ID card) Aadhaar card of the applicant and her husband PAN card of the applicant and her husband (if available) Marriage certificate/certificate issued by the Gram Panchayat or local bodies Husband's death certificate (in case of widow) Certificate issued by the society/ward/Gram Panchayat (in case of abandoned woman) Birth certificate/10th or 12th class marksheet/transfer certificate/PAN card/voter ID card/driving license (any one) Bank account details and photocopy of the bank passbook of the eligible beneficiary Self-declaration form/affidavit (attached with the application) *Applicants may be required to be present at designated locations for verification of the documents submitted with their application.

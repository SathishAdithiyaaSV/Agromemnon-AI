Scheme: Scholarships to Students Studying in Central Government Institution like IIT, IIM, IIIT, NIT and Central Universities

Description:
The scheme “Scholarships to Students Studying in Central Government Institution like IIT, IIM, IIIT, NIT and Central Universities" launched by the Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to support Tamil Nadu students from the Backward Classes (BC), Most Backward Classes (MBC), and Denotified Communities (DNC) who are pursuing undergraduate and postgraduate courses in institutions such as IITs, IIMs, IIITs, NITs, and Central Universities. Eligible students receive annual reimbursement of tuition fees, exam fees, and other compulsory fees.

Benefits:
Financial Assistance: Financial support for tuition, exam fees, and other compulsory fees up to ₹2,00,000/- annually. ﻿

Eligibility Criteria:
The beneficiary should be a student. The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu. The beneficiary should belong to the Backward Classes (BC)/ Most Backward Classes (MBC)/ Denotified Communities (DNC). The beneficiary should be pursuing UG/PG courses in IIT/ IIM/ IIIT/ NIT and Central Universities. The beneficiary's parental annual income should not exceed ₹2,50,000/-.

How to Apply:
The eligible and willing students are requested to fill out the application forms and submit the filled application to the institutions and send the filled application with the concurrence of the Principal of the Institution with all enclosures to the under-mentioned Directorate. Required Documents:
Copy of recent photograph. Birth Certificate. Community Certificate. Nativity Certificate. Income Certificate obtained after 01.04.2023 from Revenue Authorities. Copy of Aadhaar. Marks obtained copy in Entrance exam to get admission to college. Admission order copy. Bonafide certificate from the Institution. Copy of Identity card from the Institution. Academic Certificate. First page of Bank Passbook clearly showing the Account Number and IFSC Code. Fees Paid receipt copies for the year 2023-24 (All the semesters for the year 2023-24). Note: All the enclosed copies should be self-attested. ﻿

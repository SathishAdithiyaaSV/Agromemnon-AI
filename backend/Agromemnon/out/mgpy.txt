Scheme: Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana

Description:
The "Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana" launched by the Transport Department, Government of Bihar, aimed at improving transportation facilities in all panchayats of the state. It offers financial assistance to beneficiaries for purchasing vehicles.

Benefits:
Standard vehicles: 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹1,00,000/-. E-rickshaws: 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹70,000/-. Ambulances: 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹2,00,000/-.

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant must be a resident of the state. The applicant must belong to a rural area. The applicant should not have benefited from a similar scheme previously. For ambulance purchases, applicants must be willing to use it for public health services.

How to Apply:
Registration Process: Step-1: Visit the official Website . Click on the "Registration" button. Step-2: Enter your Mobile Number, New Password, Email, and Driving License No. Step-3: Click on "Register" to complete the registration process. ﻿ Application Process: Step-1: Login with your registered Mobile Number and Password. Step-2: On the Application page, fill in all the required details and upload the relevant documents. Step-3: Then click on the declaration form and click on "Submit". Step-4: Once all details are filled in correctly, click on "Print Application" to get a copy of the application form.

Required Documents:
Proof of Address. Proof of Birth. Caste Certificate. Aadhaar Details (Optional). Driving License. Marksheets of qualified exams.

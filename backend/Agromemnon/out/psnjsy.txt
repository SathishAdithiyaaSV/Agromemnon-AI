Scheme: Panchayat Samiti Nandishala Jan Sahbhagita Yojana

Description:
The "Panchayat Samiti Nandishala Jan Sahbhagita Yojana", launched in 2021-22, by the Animal Husbandry Department, Rajasthan, aims to support the construction of permanent infrastructure for the shelter of stray male cattle in each Panchayat Samiti. The scheme provides financial assistance for Nandishala.

Benefits:
One-time assistance of 90% of the total construction cost.

Eligibility Criteria:
Eligible entities include local bodies, Panchayati Raj institutions, registered trusts, NGOs, and government-recognized institutions. The organization must have been operational for at least 3 years. Ownership of 20 bighas of private land to sustain 250 male cattle. Minimum of 3 years of experience in operating a cattle shelter or similar activities. Availability of 10% contribution towards the project cost.

How to Apply:
Step 1: Obtain the application form from the Office of the District Joint Director, Animal Husbandry Department. Step 2: Review the tender notices and apply online as per the guidelines. Step 3: Submit the application form along with the required documents. Step 4: Await verification and approval from the concerned authority. ﻿ Scheme Contact Details: Nodal Officer Name & Designation: Dr. Anil Kaushik, Joint Director Phone Number: 9829332050 Email: dir.dgs@rajasthan.gov.in

Required Documents:
Proof of organization's registration and existence for at least 3 years. Land ownership documents for 20 bighas of private land. Experience certificate for operating a cattle shelter for at least 3 years. Any additional documents as per scheme guidelines.

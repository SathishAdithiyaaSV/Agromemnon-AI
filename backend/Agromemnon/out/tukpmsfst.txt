Scheme: Transgender Utthan Kosh - Post Matric Scholarship for Studying Transgender

Description:
The "Transgender Utthan Kosh - Post Matric Scholarship for Studying Transgender" scheme was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to support transgender students pursuing post-matric education. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a monthly scholarship of ₹1,000/- to cover educational expenses for their post-matric studies.

Benefits:
Post-Matric Scholarship: Scholarship @ ₹1,000/- per month per student for 10 months. NOTE: The scholarship amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary's bank account.

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant should be a resident of Rajasthan. The applicant should belong to the transgender community. The applicant should be studying at the post-matric level. The applicant should be enrolled in a recognized school/college in Rajasthan. The applicant’s family annual income should not exceed ₹8,00,000/-.

How to Apply:
Application Submission: The applicant/parents/guardians must fill out the scholarship application form and attach the required documents. Submit the complete application form to their school principal/institution. Institutional Verification: The school principal/institution verifies the application and forwards it to the Social Justice and Empowerment Department's district office. Approval Process: The district office verifies the documents and approves/rejects the application within one month. Disbursement: Upon approval, the scholarship amount is transferred to the applicant's bank account.

Required Documents:
Transgender Identity Certificate Certificate of Enrollment in a Recognized Institution/School Previous Year’s Mark Sheet Aadhaar Card Parents/Guardians Income Certificate Domicile Certificate/Ration Card/Voter ID Bank Passbook Copy Any other documents as required

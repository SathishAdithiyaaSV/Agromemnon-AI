Scheme: Financial Assistance for Funeral Expenses (JKBOCWWB)

Description:
The “Financial Assistance for Funeral Expenses” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu and Kashmir, is a welfare program for registered workers. Under this scheme, ₹5,000/- is provided as funeral assistance to the nominee or legal heir upon the death of the registered worker.

Benefits:
₹5,000/- is provided as funeral assistance.

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant must be the nominee or legal heir of the deceased worker. The deceased worker must have been registered with the Jammu and Kashmir Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. The deceased worker must have been an active member of the Board at the time of death. The scheme can be applied for only once.

How to Apply:
Application - Step 1: Go to the official website of the Jammu & Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Step 2: Click on "Login," enter your details, and sign in to your account. Step 3: After logging in, click on "Scheme Registration" and select "Apply for Scheme." Step 4: Choose the scheme you wish to apply for in order to avail the benefits. Step 5: Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted. Step 6: Finally, click on "Submit Application" to complete the process. ﻿ Status Tracking - Applicant can track their application status online through the official website of the  Jammu & Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number.

Required Documents:
Identity Card of the Deceased Worker Identity Proof of the Nominee/Legal Heir Certificate of Being a Nominee/legal Heir Passport-size Photograph Death Certificate Undertaking Any Other Documents as Required

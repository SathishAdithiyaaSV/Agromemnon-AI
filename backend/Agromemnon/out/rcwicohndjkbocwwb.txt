Scheme: Registered Construction Worker in case of his/her Natural Death (JKBOCWWB)

Description:
Launched on 1st January 2018, the scheme "Registered Construction Worker in Case of His/Her Natural Death" is a Welfare Scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,00,000/- is provided to the nominee or dependents in the event of natural death of a registered BOC worker. The applications are accepted online as well as offline.

Benefits:
₹2,00,000/- is provided to the nominee/dependent of the deceased construction worker.

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant should be a nominee/dependent of a registered member/worker who has deceased due to natural causes.

How to Apply:
Application - Step 1: Go to the official website of the Jammu & Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Step 2: Click on "Login," enter your details, and sign in to your account. Step 3: After logging in, click on "Scheme Registration" and select "Apply for Scheme." Step 4: Choose the scheme you wish to apply for in order to avail the benefits. Step 5: Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted. Step 6: Finally, click on "Submit Application" to complete the process. ﻿ Status Tracking - Applicant can track their application status online through the official website of the  Jammu & Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number.

Required Documents:
Original Death Certificate of the Worker. Attested Age Proof of the Worker. Duly attested photocopy of Bank Passbook of the nominee/legal heir or a cancelled cheque bearing the name of the nominee/legal heir and the IFSC code of the bank branch. Photocopy of the Aadhaar Card or Biometric Card of the deceased. Photocopy of the Aadhaar Card or Biometric Card of the nominee/claimant.

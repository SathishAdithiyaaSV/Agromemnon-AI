Scheme: Post Matric College Scholarship (Scheduled Caste)

Description:
Launched in 1976-77, the "Post Matric College Scholarship" is a scheme by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides financial assistance to encourage Scheduled Caste students to pursue higher education. One-third of the subsistence allowance is payable to students residing in departmental hostels who are provided free lodging and boarding facilities.

Benefits:
Post-matric students are provided with scholarships.

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant must be a student. The student should belong to the Scheduled Caste category. The student must come from a family with an income limit of up to ₹6.00 lakh to qualify. ﻿ Note: One-third of the subsistence allowance will be payable to students residing in departmental hostels who are provided with free lodging/boarding facilities. ﻿ ﻿ ﻿

How to Apply:
Registration Process: Step1: Go to the official State Scholarship Portal 2.0 for Madhya Pradesh. Step2: Scroll down the page and find the "Student Corner" section on the left side of the screen. Step3: Under the Student Corner, click on the “Student Registration” option. Step4: Complete the online registration form by filling in all mandatory fields and uploading the required documents in the specified format and size. Step5: Carefully review the details entered and the documents uploaded. Once you have verified everything, click on the "Submit" button. Step6: After submitting, you will receive your registration number on the registered email address or mobile number. ﻿ Application Process: Step1: Go to the State Scholarship Portal 2.0 for Madhya Pradesh. Step2: Scroll down to the "Student Corner" section on the left and click on "Student Login." Enter your User ID and Password to log in. Step3: After logging in, a dashboard will appear in front of you. From your dashboard, select the scheme you wish to apply for and click on it. Step4: Complete the application form by filling in all required fields and uploading necessary documents in the specified format and size. Step5: Double-check the information and documents provided, then click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.

Required Documents:
Aadhaar Card Caste Certificate Income Certificate Bank Account Details Passport-sized Photograph Educational Documents Self-attested Documents Proof of Admission Income Declaration Bank Details ﻿

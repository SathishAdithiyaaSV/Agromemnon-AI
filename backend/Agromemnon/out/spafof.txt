Scheme: Scheme of Providing Assistance to Encourage Farmers for Adoption of Organic Farming

Description:
The "Scheme of Providing Assistance to Encourage Farmers for Adoption of Organic Farming" introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to encourage farmers to adopt organic farming by offering financial incentives for certification, registration, and input costs. It helps maintain soil fertility, supports biological input management, and promotes traditional knowledge.

Benefits:
Name of the Component Standard of Assistance Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming  Benefits Registration/ Certification Assistance for Certification/Registration fees 75% from GOPCA and 50% assistance in Certification fees with other certifying Body;  Upto Maximum  ₹2,000/- per Hectare Every year Input Input subsidy of ₹5,000/- per Hectare for Certified farm Every year Residue testing Per sample Assistance upto ₹1,0000/- Every year ﻿

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant must be an Individual farmer or farmers group. The applicant should have an interest in practicing certified organic farming system.

How to Apply:
Step 1: The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/ ﻿ Step 2: On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’. Step 3: Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open. Step 4: Click on "Apply New" button and submit a new application. Step 5: Click on the "Update Application" button to add corrections to the application. Step 6: Once the application is done, confirm it. Step 7: Take a print out of the confirmed application and submit Related Documents through to concern Taluka office. Step 8: Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx  Contact Us:  Click here .

Required Documents:
Aadhaar Card. Bank Passbook. Land ownership proof. Organic certification documents (if available). Recent Passport Size Photograph. Any other document if required.

Scheme: Transgender Utthan Kosh - Pre Matric Scholarship for Studying Transgender

Description:
The "Transgender Utthan Kosh - Pre Matric Scholarship for Studying Transgender" scheme was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to support transgender students by providing scholarships to encourage their education and social upliftment. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a monthly scholarship to cover educational expenses from primary to pre-matric levels.

Benefits:
Pre-Matric Scholarship: ₹225/- per month per student for 10 months. NOTE: The scholarship amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary's bank account.

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant should be a resident of Rajasthan. The applicant should belong to the transgender community. The applicant should be studying at the primary to pre-matric level. The applicant should be enrolled in a recognized school within Rajasthan. The applicant’s family annual income should not exceed ₹8,00,000/-.

How to Apply:
Application Submission: The applicant/parents/guardians must fill out the scholarship application form and attach the required documents. Submit the complete application form to their school principal. Institutional Verification: The school principal verifies the application and forwards it to the Social Justice and Empowerment Department's district office. Approval Process: The district office verifies the documents and approves/rejects the application within one month. Disbursement: Upon approval, the scholarship amount is transferred to the applicant's bank account.

Required Documents:
Transgender Identity Certificate School Enrollment Certificate Previous Year’s Mark Sheet Aadhaar Card Parents/Guardians Income Certificate Domicile Certificate/Ration Card/Voter ID Bank Passbook Copy Any other documents as required

Scheme: Soil Testing Laboratory

Description:
Description Soil Testing Lab - For analysing soil samples preceding the application of fertilizer in the correct dosage in farmers' field. Objectives The analysis is free of cost of soil samples preceding application of fertilizers/ liming etc. for farmers along with distribution of Soil Health Card (SHC). Mode of Selection of Beneficiary(s) Bonafide farmers have sufficient available cultivable land on a first come first served basis.

Benefits:
To provide soil testing services for farmers free of cost. Soil Health Card (SHC).

Eligibility Criteria:
Bonafide farmers engaged in Agriculture

How to Apply:
Bonafide farmers can apply in plain paper through the nearest Agriculture Development Officer of a CD Block/Agriculture Circle.

Required Documents:
Farmer’s Photo. Verification Form by the Agriculture Development Officer of a CD Block/Agriculture Circle. Bank Details (Account No., IFSC Code) of the applicant. Aadhaar Number (or alternative identification documents. ﻿

Level: State | Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment

Scheme: Running of Scheduled Caste Hostel

Description:
The "Running of Scheduled Caste Hostels" scheme by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to provide housing and a conducive study environment for Scheduled Caste students. Separate junior, senior, and college hostels are operated for boys and girls, offering free accommodation and food facilities to support their education.

Benefits:
Scholarship, Free Food and Accommodation, Sports Equipment, Toiletry Kit, and Library Facilities. 90% of the scholarship amount is paid in the joint account of the hostel superintendent and mess secretary (student) for running the mess. 10% is paid in the student's account.

Eligibility Criteria:
The applicant should be a Student. The applicant should be from a Scheduled Caste. The applicant should be a native of Madhya Pradesh. The applicant should be enrolled in a recognized institution. *The applicant should note that there is no income limit for admission to senior hostels.

How to Apply:
Application Step 1: Obtain the application form from the Divisional District Office or the Designated Hostel. Step 2: Fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Step 3: Submit the completed application to the concerned hostel within the stipulated deadline fixed by the District President. Ensure the application is forwarded by the Head of the Institution before submission. Verification and Approval Step 1: The concerned Divisional District Officer will verify the application and supporting documents. Step 12: Upon approval, admission will be granted based on hostel capacity and compliance with prerequisites. Timeline Applications must be submitted within one month after the date fixed for admission in schools.

Required Documents:
Passport-size Photograph Aadhaar card Domicile Certificate Caste Certificate Previous Class Passing Certificate / Marksheet Samagra ID Bank account Details
